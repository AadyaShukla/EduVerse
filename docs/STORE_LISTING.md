# EduVerse - Google Play Store Listing Metadata

## 1. App Title & Descriptions

### App Title (30 chars max)
`EduVerse: AI Study Assistant`

### Short Description (80 chars max)
`AI doubt solver, adaptive quizzes, interactive lectures & study tools for K-12.`

### Full Description
```text
EduVerse is your all-in-one AI-powered learning companion designed specifically for K-12 students. 

WHETHER YOU ARE SOLVING COMPLEX MATH PROBLEMS, REVISING SCIENCE CONCEPTS, OR ORGANIZING YOUR DAILY STUDY TIMETABLE, EDUVERSE HAS YOU COVERED.

KEY FEATURES:
• AI Doubt Solver: Take a photo of any printed or handwritten question or speak your doubt. Get instant, step-by-step step explanations in your choice of language (English, Hindi, Spanish, French & more).
• Interactive AI Lectures: On-demand slide-based lectures narrated aloud. Pause at any time to ask doubts and test your knowledge with embedded checkpoint questions.
• Adaptive Quizzes & Mock Exams: Test your knowledge with AI-generated quizzes that automatically adjust difficulty based on your performance. Track weak topics and revise using spaced repetition.
• Productivity Tools: Keep your notes organized with automatic subject tagging, scan handwritten notes via ML Kit OCR, and manage class timetables with local reminders.
• Pomodoro Focus Mode & Study Receipts: Stay engaged with built-in focus timers, earn XP, build daily streaks, unlock badges, and receive daily study summary receipts.
• Safe & Private: No ads, no third-party tracking, and full parent/guardian transparency for student wellbeing.
```
