# EduVerse Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: August 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction & Overview
EduVerse ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting student privacy, safety, and digital wellbeing. This Privacy Policy outlines our data collection, usage, storage, and parental rights practices for our K-12 learning application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect only the minimum data required to deliver personalized AI-driven education:
- **Student Profile**: Name, Grade level, and local security PIN.
- **Learning Data**: Typed or OCR-scanned doubts, generated quiz responses, study notes, timetable entries, and focus session durations.
- **Parent/Guardian Data**: Name and email address (for account verification and 1-on-1 progress summary).

We **DO NOT** collect:
- Precise geolocation data
- Biometric raw templates (biometric authentication is processed strictly on-device via Android Keystore APIs)
- Device contacts, SMS, or browsing history
- Identifiers for advertising or tracking

---

## 3. How We Use Information
Student data is strictly used to:
1. Personalize AI doubt explanations, lectures, and adaptive quizzes.
2. Track daily study streaks, XP, and weak topics.
3. Send study deadline reminders via on-device local notifications.
4. Provide a 1-on-1 weekly summary insight to the linked parent or guardian.

**Zero Advertising**: We do not display ads, participate in ad networks, or build user profiles for behavioral targeting.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
- **Grade < 7 Mandatory Guardian Link**: Students under grade 7 require parent/guardian verification to activate their account.
- **Parental Rights**: Parents can request access to or permanent deletion of their child's data at any time through the in-app "Delete My Account & Data" option or by contacting privacy@eduverse.app.

---

## 5. Security & Data Retention
All data transmitted between the app and our backend is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 1.3. Service credentials and AI keys are securely stored in server-side environment variables and are never embedded in client software.

Students or guardians can permanently erase all account records and learning history at any time using the "Delete My Account & Data" button in Settings.
